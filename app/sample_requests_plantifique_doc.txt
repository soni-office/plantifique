Plantifique TikTok Shop Sample Request Processing Policy

---

TIER SYSTEM — STRATEGIC CLASSIFICATION

Tier 1: Retainers (Priority One)
Definition: Creators currently under contract or on a long-term retainer.
Action: Bot Bypass. These requests are flagged for the internal team to handle (white-glove service). Do NOT evaluate through standard criteria.

Tier 2: Exception List
Definition: Creators requiring manual review, typically high-value relationships managed via Discord/DMs.
Action: Bot Bypass. Flagged for internal team review only. Do NOT score automatically.

Tier 3: Q1 Strategic Focus Products
Products: Peach Foot Mask, Turmeric Cleansing Pads, Marine Clay Mask, V-Line Mask, Jade Gua Sha
Evaluation: Apply performance-based vetting using US Sample Approval Criteria (Follower/GMV thresholds + Aesthetic Fit).
Scoring: Bot evaluates and scores creator-product compatibility.

Tier 4: Legacy / Q4 Products (Open Plan)
Products: Mango Cleansing Balm, Kojic Acid Bar Soap
Context: No longer proactively scouted. Still active and receiving organic requests.
Evaluation: Reject if creator does not meet baseline criteria — preserve inventory.
Exception: Approve if creator has high historical ROI specifically with our brand.

Tier 5: All Other Products
Action: Flag for internal review only. No strict performance metrics applied. White-glove service by internal team.

---

AESTHETIC STANDARDS — PROFILE & CONTENT AUDIT

These standards apply to all Tier 3 and Tier 4 creator evaluations.

Technical Content Audit:
- Posting Cadence: Creator must post at least 3–5 times per week.
- Recency: Must have posted within the last 7 days.
- Content Type: Feed should feature "Get Ready With Me" (GRWM), ASMR, educational, or engaging beauty/lifestyle content.

Visual & Aesthetic Standards (Clean Beauty):
- Lighting: Bright, natural, or soft-box lighting. Reject grainy or dark "bedroom" videos.
- Environment: Clean backgrounds — organized vanities, spa-like bathrooms.
- Production: High-resolution (4K preferred) close-ups of skin and product texture. Minimal heavy blur filters.

A mismatch in aesthetic quality (e.g., poor lighting, cluttered backgrounds) is a negative signal for Tier 3/4 approvals.

---

HISTORICAL COLLABORATION AUDIT

Every creator who is NOT on the Retainer List must be evaluated against their collaboration history. This prevents "sample hustling" and rewards loyalty.

Case 1: Past Sample + Generated GMV
Action: High Priority Approval. Past sales generation is a proven asset indicator.
Note: Approve even if slightly below current GMV threshold for secondary products.

Case 2: Past Sample + NO GMV (no sales, no content posted)
Action: Automatic Rejection. Do not send a second sample to a creator who failed to perform.

Case 3: No Past Sample
Action: Standard Vetting. Evaluate strictly on US Sample Approval Criteria (Followers, 30-day GMV, Aesthetic Fit).

---

APPROVAL DECISION LOGIC

Approve: Creator is a Past Success (generated GMV), OR a Prospect meeting Focus Product criteria for Tier 3/4.
Reject: Creator is a Past Failure (received sample, no GMV) OR a Prospect failing to meet Tier 3/4 threshold criteria.
Flag Internal: Creator is a Retainer (Tier 1), on the Exception List (Tier 2), or requesting a non-Focus product (Tier 5).

---

US SAMPLE APPROVAL CRITERIA (Tier 3 / Tier 4)

Primary filters evaluated before AI scoring:
1. Minimum GMV: Must meet product-specific GMV floor (indicates proven sales capability).
2. Minimum Follower Count: Baseline reach requirement per product.
3. Minimum Post Rate: Minimum fulfillment/posting percentage to ensure content will actually be created.

Creators passing all three filters proceed to full AI compatibility scoring (aesthetic fit, audience alignment, category match).
